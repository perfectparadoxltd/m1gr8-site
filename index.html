// Questions
Does it go both ways?

Yes. Notebound moves Markdown into Apple Notes and exports your Apple Notes back out to clean .md files. Pick a direction and convert — the formatting is preserved either way.

Can I export my Apple Notes to files?

Yes — this is the part Apple never built. Apple Notes has no “export to folder,” so Notebound reads your notes for you and writes one tidy Markdown file per note: headings, lists, checklists, code and links intact, folder structure recreated where it can. The result is a plain .md folder you can open in Obsidian, back up, or commit to git.

Why Markdown for AI?

Markdown is what Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and local models read natively and efficiently — no tokens or context burned on Apple’s proprietary note format. Once your Apple Notes are clean .md files, you can hand the whole folder to any AI, index it, or feed it to a local model — your Apple brain, finally readable by your tools.

What formatting does it preserve?

Headings, bold and italic, ordered and unordered lists, checklists, code (inline and blocks), real tables with borders, links and images — in both directions. It speaks the Obsidian dialect too — frontmatter is dropped cleanly and tags carry over. The goal is your notes look the same on the other side as they did before.

Where do my notes end up?

Wherever you point it. Going in, every converted note lands in Apple Notes on your Mac — folder structure recreated where it can, syncing to your other Apple devices through iCloud like any other note. Going out, you get a folder of .md files on disk, ready to use anywhere.

How does the free model work?

Notebound is free to use with a built-in limit on how many notes a single conversion moves — and there's no time limit, ever. You can always analyse your whole library for free, in either direction, to see exactly what will be preserved or changed. Buying once unlocks unlimited, full conversions both ways.

Will it modify my existing notes or files?

No. Notebound only ever reads your source — whether that's Markdown files or Apple Notes — and only ever creates new things: new notes in Apple Notes, or new .md files on disk. It never edits or deletes what you already have. Your originals stay exactly as they were, in both directions.

What happens to my wikilinks?

Apple Notes has no public API for linking one note to another, so navigable note-to-note links aren't possible. Notebound converts your [[wikilinks]] into clear text references instead, so you can see what was linked — we'd rather be honest than pretend they're clickable.

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time purchase of $9.99, yours forever. No recurring fees, no account, no renewals. Pay once and convert as much as you like, in either direction.

Does Notebound upload my notes?

Never. Everything runs on your Mac — there's no account, no sign-up and zero telemetry, and Notebound makes no network calls at all. Your notes never leave the machine, going in or coming out. Your “AI-ready” .md folder stays yours; you decide if and when it ever touches an AI.

Does it work offline?

Completely. All conversion happens on-device, so Notebound works with the network unplugged. No cloud, no servers, no dependency on anyone's API — just your Mac doing the work.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — a 14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. And since you can analyse your vault for free before paying, you'll know what to expect first. Payments are handled by Paddle; just reply to your receipt and we'll sort it out.
