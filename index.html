// Questions
What formatting does it preserve?

Headings, bold and italic, ordered and unordered lists, checklists, code (inline and blocks), real tables with borders, links and images. It speaks the Obsidian dialect too — frontmatter is dropped cleanly and tags carry over. The goal is your notes look the same in Apple Notes as they did in Markdown.

Where do my notes end up?

In Apple Notes, on your Mac. Notebound recreates your folder structure where it can, and lands every converted note inside Notes ready to use — and syncing to your other Apple devices through iCloud just like any other note.

How does the free model work?

Notebound is free to use with a built-in limit on how many notes a single migration moves — and there's no time limit, ever. You can always analyse your whole vault for free to see exactly what will be preserved or changed. Buying once unlocks unlimited, full migrations.

Will it modify my existing notes or files?

No. Notebound never touches your Markdown source — it reads it, that's all. And it only ever creates new notes in Apple Notes; it doesn't edit or delete anything you already have there. Your originals stay exactly as they were.

What happens to my wikilinks?

Apple Notes has no public API for linking one note to another, so navigable note-to-note links aren't possible. Notebound converts your [[wikilinks]] into clear text references instead, so you can see what was linked — we'd rather be honest than pretend they're clickable.

Is it a subscription?

No. One-time purchase of $9.99, yours forever. No recurring fees, no account, no renewals. Pay once and migrate as much as you like.

What about my privacy?

Everything runs on your Mac. There's no account, no sign-up and zero telemetry — Notebound makes no network calls at all. Your notes never leave the machine.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — a 14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. And since you can analyse your vault for free before paying, you'll know what to expect first. Payments are handled by Paddle; just reply to your receipt and we'll sort it out.
